Cook what you already have

Recipe Assistant searches nearly 5,000 real recipes by what you actually mean — not by keyword. Tell it “something quick and gluten free with chicken”, or photograph your fridge shelf, and it finds dishes that fit. Then it remembers: what's in your cupboard, what you plan to cook this week, and what you still need to buy.

🔍

Search in plain language

Describe the meal you want. Dietary needs and time limits are pulled out of your sentence automatically, so “vegan pasta under 30 minutes” filters as well as it reads.

📷

Search with a photo

Point your camera at what's in the fridge. The app identifies the ingredients it can see and searches with them — useful when you know what you have but not what to make.

🧺

Your pantry, remembered

Save the ingredients you keep at home. Results then show how many of them each recipe uses, and the ones using the most come first.

🗓️

Plan the week

Drop recipes into a seven-day, three-meal grid. Unlike asking a chatbot, the plan is still there on Thursday.

🛒

Shopping list that does the maths

The list is your planned recipes' ingredients minus what's already in your pantry. Check items off as you shop, add anything else by hand.

❤️

Save and organise

Keep the recipes you like and group them into named collections — weeknight dinners, things to try, whatever fits how you cook.
