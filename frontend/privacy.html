Privacy & data

What this app stores, who it talks to, and how to get rid of it. Written in plain language rather than legal boilerplate.

What we store

Only what the app needs to work. Specifically:

  • Your email address — this is your account identity. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; if you use a password, it is set and checked by Firebase and never reaches our server.
  • Recipes you save, and the collections you group them into.
  • Your pantry list — the ingredient names you add.
  • Your meal plan — which recipe you put in which day and meal.
  • Your shopping list state — which items you ticked off and anything you added by hand. The list itself is not stored; it is recalculated from your plan and pantry each time you open it.

We do not ask for or store your name, address, phone number, or any payment details. There is no payment in this app.

Searches and photos

When you search, your query is sent to Google's Gemini API so it can judge whether it is a food request and write a short note about the results. When you use camera search, the photo is sent to the same API to identify visible ingredients.

Photos are not saved. They are sent, processed, and discarded — nothing is written to our database. Search queries are not stored against your account either.

Who else is involved

  • Google Firebase — sign-in and the database holding the items listed above.
  • Google Gemini — processes search text and camera photos as described.
  • Render and Vercel — host the server and the website.
  • Amazon — only when you choose to open a shopping link (see below).

We do not sell your data, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers on the site.

Affiliate links

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. The shopping links in the app carry an affiliate tag, which lets Amazon attribute a purchase to this site and pay a small commission.

Using them costs you nothing extra, and the link is a normal product search — we are not paid to recommend particular items, and the recipes you see are never influenced by it. If you would rather not use them, search the store yourself; nothing in the app depends on it.

Once you follow such a link, you are on Amazon's site and their privacy policy applies, not ours.

Deleting your data

Anything you added, you can remove in the app: unsave recipes, delete collections, clear your pantry, clear a week of your plan.

To delete your account and everything attached to it, email gdeniznarin6@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and it will be removed.

One honest caveat

This is a student project, not a commercial service. It is built with care — passwords are handled by Firebase, the API is locked to this site's origin, and recipe data is read-only — but it does not carry the guarantees, support, or uptime commitments of a production product. Dietary tags are generated automatically and are best-effort estimates: if you have an allergy, always read the ingredient list yourself.
